# Unscroll — Support

Questions, bug reports, or anything else about the app: **m.bows@outlook.com**. We aim to respond within a few days.

## Common questions

**How do I delete my account?**
Open Unscroll → Settings → Account → Delete account. This is immediate and permanent.

**How do I cancel my subscription?**
Subscriptions are managed by Apple, not inside Unscroll. On your iPhone: Settings → your name → Subscriptions → Unscroll → Cancel Subscription.

**I paid but Premium isn't showing up.**
Open Unscroll → Settings → Premium → Restore Purchases.
